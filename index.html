Fíjate que no necesitas memorizar 8 estructuras diferentes. Varias se repiten.
Planes → What are your plans for...?
1️⃣ What are your plans for after the test?
5️⃣ What are your plans for tonight?
Pasado → What did you do...?
2️⃣ What did you do on your last birthday?
6️⃣ What did you do last summer?
8️⃣ What did you do yesterday?
Lugar en pasado → Where did you go...?
3️⃣ Where did you go on your last vacation?
7️⃣ Where did you go on your last trip?
Si dominas What are your plans...? / What did you do...? / Where did you go...?, ya tienes 7 de las 8 bastante encaminadas.
Answer these questions — Responde estas preguntas. Debajo de cada pregunta está su significado.
9
Pregunta
What do you do in your free time?
🇨🇱 ¿Qué haces en tu tiempo libre?
🔊 Uát du yu dú in yor frí táim?
Respuesta
I play video games and watch movies.
🔊 Ai pléi vídeo gueims and uách múvis.
10
Pregunta
Describe your personality with three adjectives. Give reasons for the adjectives.
🇨🇱 Describe tu personalidad con tres adjetivos y da razones.
🔊 Diskráib yor personáliti uiz zrí ádyectivs. Guiv rízons for di ádyectivs.
Respuesta
I am friendly because I like helping people. I am responsible because I work and study. I am hardworking because I always try to do my best.
🔊 Ai am fréndli bicóz ai láik jélping pípol. Ai am rispónsabol bicóz ai uérk and stádi. Ai am járd-uérking bicóz ai ólweis trai tu dú mai best.

Pregunta

🔊 Nekst yír, ai plan tu stádi and uérk.

Pregunta
